The ask
•Approve £194m at Gate 3, set at P80
•Includes £18m risk allowance and £12m management reserve, held separately
The case
•The 1974 works cannot meet the 2029 treatment standard
•Serves 214,000 customers; no spare capacity if a stream fails

•New membrane plant on the adjacent site scores 3.2 of 4
•Rebuilding on the current site scores 2.6 and takes 14 months longer

•Ground conditions and membrane supply drive most of the uncertainty
•Without the extra £12m, the budget has only a 50% chance of holding

•Four of four closed: planning consent, land purchase, design freeze, supplier price check
