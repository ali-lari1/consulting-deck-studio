Tenants wait too long because nine directorates
share each decision
•A repair needing two directorates takes 23 days on average
•Six management layers separate tenants from the Chief Executive
Four principles guide the design
•One owner per tenant outcome
•Decisions taken close to tenants
•Fewer layers
•Common services shared
The target model has five divisions and five layers
•Average span of control rises from 4.1 to 7.0
•Headcount falls from 1,240 to 1,150 FTE: 60 vacancies and 30 voluntary exits
The new structure goes live on 1 July 2027
•Costs £3.1m to achieve; payback in 8 months from full run rate
•Repairs and tenant contact keep running throughout
Slide 2. The saving is £4.8m a year at full run rate, which is 90 posts. 60 come from vacancies we would not refill and 30 from a voluntary exit window.
