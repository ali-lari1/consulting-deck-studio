# Consulting Deck Studio

When the user asks for a consulting deck, a SteerCo or board pack, a project or programme status report, a RAG
dashboard, a risk review, a gate paper or business case, a cost or schedule review, a diagnostic, an operating model,
a transformation roadmap or a strategy deck, or asks to turn meeting notes, transcripts, schedules or registers into
slides, activate the `consulting-deck-studio` skill (in `skills/consulting-deck-studio/`) and follow its SKILL.md.
