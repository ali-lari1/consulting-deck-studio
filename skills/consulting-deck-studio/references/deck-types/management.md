# Management consulting deck types

Operations, cost, organisation and transformation work. The inputs are typically finance and activity data, HR data
(headcount, spans and layers), process maps, interview notes and benchmarks. Every saving number needs a baseline, a
basis (recurrent or one-off, gross or net, cashable or not) and a phasing.

---

## 1. Operational diagnostic
**Purpose:** find where cost, time or quality is lost, and size it. **Length:** 12-20 slides.

**Default sequence:**
1. `exec-summary`
2. `key-numbers`
3. `issue-tree` (the hypotheses and their status)
4. `driver-tree` (the cost or performance metric broken into drivers; check the arithmetic)
5. Evidence slides per driver: `bar`, `benchmark`, `line`
6. `quotes` (interviews)
7. `waterfall`: the sized opportunity by lever
8. `columns`: what it would take
9. `process-flow` (next steps)

**Mandatory:** the baseline year and scope, the opportunity as a range with its basis, and the interview and data
sources.

**Pitfalls:** opportunity sizes with no stated basis, and benchmarks against a hand-picked peer set.

## 2. Cost reduction / efficiency review
**Default sequence:**
1. `exec-summary` (the target, the identified savings and the gap)
2. `waterfall`: baseline cost to target, by lever
3. `marimekko` or `bar`: spend by category and function
4. `benchmark`
5. `options-matrix` (levers against size, ease, risk and time)
6. `roadmap` (phasing)
7. `benefits-tracker` (once in delivery)
8. `decisions`

**Mandatory:** gross against net savings, one-off costs to achieve, phasing by year, owners per lever, and the
treatment of double counting across levers.

## 3. Operating model and organisation design
**Default sequence:**
1. `exec-summary`
2. `columns`: design principles (agreed first)
3. `before-after` (today against the target operating model, by dimension)
4. `org-chart` (the target structure)
5. `raci` (decision rights)
6. `table` (spans and layers or headcount by function, today against target)
7. `roadmap` (transition)
8. `risk-heatmap` (people and service risks)
9. `decisions`

**Mandatory:** principles traceable to the design, the headcount effect stated (and handled with HR and consultation
rules in mind), and service continuity.

**Pitfalls:**
- org charts with names before the design is approved
- no decision rights, only boxes

## 4. Transformation roadmap and business case
**Default sequence:**
1. `exec-summary`
2. `key-numbers` (value at stake, investment, payback)
3. `issue-tree` or `columns` (the workstreams, MECE)
4. `roadmap` (waves)
5. `waterfall` (value bridge by workstream)
6. `line` (cumulative cash: investment against benefit, showing payback)
7. `org-chart` (governance)
8. `risk-heatmap`
9. `decisions`

**Mandatory:** value phasing, investment by year, the dependencies between waves, and the governance and benefits
owners.

## 5. Transformation office SteerCo update
Use the PMO status report (`pmo.md` section 1), with a value lens:
- `benefits-tracker` (savings delivered against plan, by lever)
- `waterfall` (plan → delivered → at risk → gap)
- a `rag-dashboard` by workstream

**Mandatory:** delivered, banked and forecast savings shown separately, with an audit trail (the source system).

## 6. Post-merger integration (PMI)
**Default sequence:**
1. `exec-summary`
2. `key-numbers` (the synergy target, the cost to achieve, the Day-1 date)
3. `timeline` (Day 1, Day 100, end state)
4. `table` (Day-1 must-haves with RAG)
5. `waterfall` (synergies by type)
6. `org-chart` (the Integration Management Office)
7. `raid-log` (interdependencies)
8. `decisions`

**Mandatory:** clean-team and competition-law constraints respected (no sharing of competitively sensitive data
before close), the synergy basis and phasing, and Day-1 criteria.

## 7. Public-sector service review
**Default sequence:**
1. `key-numbers`
2. `benchmark` (cost or performance per unit against comparable authorities)
3. `process-flow` (the current journey with its pain points)
4. `quotes` (staff and residents, by role)
5. `before-after` (service model)
6. `options-matrix`
7. `roadmap`
8. `decisions`

**Mandatory:** comparators named with their criteria, the statutory duties respected, and an equality impact noted
where relevant.

## 8. Change and adoption plan
**Default sequence:**
1. `table` (stakeholder impact by group: what changes, how much, and when)
2. `matrix-2x2` (stakeholders on influence against support)
3. `roadmap` (communications and training)
4. `line` or `table` (adoption KPIs)
5. `raci`

**Pitfalls:** "communications" as the whole change plan, and no adoption measures.
